# 权利问题反馈 / Rights inquiries / 権利に関する連絡

[中文](../README.md) · [English](../README.en.md) · [日本語](../README.ja.md)

## 中文

如果认为本仓库包含未经许可的内容，或来源、版权、商标表述有误，请通过本仓库的 **Issues** 提供文件路径、涉及段落、原始来源、权利依据及希望采取的处理方式。仅需足够定位问题的信息；不要公开个人身份证件、私人联系方式、未经许可的 APK/LUT 或签名密钥。Issues 是公开的。

维护者会评估具体问题，并视情况更正说明、移除内容或暂停相关分发。这不构成保证，也不限制权利人依法采取其他措施。正式版权投诉可使用 [GitHub 的版权投诉流程](https://docs.github.com/en/site-policy/content-removal-policies/guide-to-submitting-a-dmca-takedown-notice)。

不要在 Issue 中请求绕过付费应用授权、分享第三方 APK 或重新上传官方 LUT。本项目维护者无权代 Sony、FUJIFILM、Ricoh 或其他权利人授予许可。

## English

If you believe content lacks permission, or an attribution, copyright or trademark statement is inaccurate, use this repository's **Issues** to identify the file, passage, original source, basis of your rights and requested action. Provide only what is needed to locate the issue. Do not post identity documents, private contact details, unlicensed APK/LUT files or signing keys. Issues are public.

Maintainers will assess specific concerns and may correct notices, remove content or suspend relevant distribution. This is not a guarantee and does not limit a rights holder's legal remedies. Formal copyright notices can follow [GitHub's copyright process](https://docs.github.com/en/site-policy/content-removal-policies/guide-to-submitting-a-dmca-takedown-notice).

Do not request paid-app authorization bypasses, third-party APK sharing or official LUT reuploads. Maintainers cannot grant permission on behalf of Sony, FUJIFILM, Ricoh or other rights holders.
